
Question 1 (CA): Explain the purpose of interatomic potentials in MD. I would then like you to
find 3 different types of interatomic potentials than what was discussed in class and do the
following: (a) write and label their mathematical form to predict the total potential energy, (b)
determine what types of materials the potential is designed for (ex: metals) and explain why, and
(c) explain the potential pitfalls of using this potential to run MD.

Interatomic potentials in MD determine the energeis of the atoms based on their distance from each other (and other factors depending on the potential type) and is the main calculation at every time step. The (negative) gradient of your energies is the force which gives acceleration and an iteration uses those to update the atoms positions.  Using interatomic potentials instead of DFT will cost you some accuracy, but it buys you speed.
