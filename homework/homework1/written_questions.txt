  5C.   Explain in 2-4 sentences why the scaler must be fit using only the
        training data.

            This prevents data leakage; to accurately asses the ability of a model to extrapolate to unseen data you need to train only on one set, and evaluate on a held out, never seen during training set. 
            scaling on data that includes what you are evaluating on is "cheating" in the sense that you gleen information from data that you are assuming you don't have access to.
